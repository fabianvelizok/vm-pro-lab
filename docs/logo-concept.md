# VM Pro Lab — Logo Concept

**The Venn theorem, turned into a mark.**

VM Pro Lab is built by two people — **V** for **Veliz** and **M** for **Mallorga**. The logo tells that story with the most honest figure possible: a **Venn diagram**.

## The concept

Two circles that meet. Each one is a person; where they cross is what they build together. It's the visual translation of the idea that defines the studio — *"Two developers. One system of work."* Not two separate services: one intersection.

## What each part means

**The two circles — the two people.**
Each circle is one of the founders. Each is complete on their own (their own world, their own expertise), but they choose to meet. The Venn diagram isn't decorative — it's pure logic, fitting for a studio that builds systems.

**The intersection — the partnership.**
The center, where the circles overlap, is what exists *only because they are together*: the team, the method, the products. It's *"two developers, one system of work"* made visible. The most valuable thing about the brand isn't either of them alone — it's what appears in the middle.

**The hatched intersection — the work / the "Lab".**
In set theory, the intersection is shaded to say *"this is the shared set."* That hatching gives the mark a technical, blueprint texture: it marks that *there*, at the crossing, is where the work is concentrated.

**The two nodes — the meeting points.**
The small dots where the circles touch are the exact points of connection. Precision and intent: two paths that cross at a defined place, not by chance.

**The letters V and M — the names.**
They aren't abstract letters — they are **Veliz** and **Mallorga**. The logo is, literally, the two people. Each letter lives inside its circle and carries its color, because each side is its own identity.

## What the colors mean

**Cyan — M (Mallorga).** The brightest, most electric tone in the palette. The spark: energy, the digital, freshness, clarity.

**Blue — V (Veliz).** A deep, dependable azure: the classic color of technology and engineering. Solidity, craft, trust.

**The two together — the brand spectrum.** Cyan + blue is the studio's "plasma" range — the one associated with technology and AI, repeated across the whole site (buttons, accents). Where they mix — in the intersection — the two colors **fuse**: the union is, literally, the sum of the two.

**The balance.** Both colors are tuned to carry the same visual weight — neither dominates. A partnership of equals, 50/50.

## Built to scale

The same symbol works huge in the hero and tiny as a favicon, on dark or light backgrounds — 100% vector (a lightweight, dependency-free SVG). The favicon is a simplified version (the two rings + the intersection, without the letters, which dissolve at small sizes).

> **In one line:** two people, two colors, one intersection — the Venn theorem as VM Pro Lab's signature.
